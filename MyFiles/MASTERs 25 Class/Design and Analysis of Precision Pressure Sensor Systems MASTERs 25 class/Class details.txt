Title:
Design and Analysis of Precision Pressure Sensor Systems
 
Learning Objectives
Modeling Pressure Sensors: The student will utilize parameters from pressure sensor data sheets to accurately model sensors in MPLAB Mindi. This includes understanding the key specifications of pressure sensors, such as sensitivity, range, and accuracy, and how these parameters influence the sensor's performance in different applications.
Designing and Analyzing Topologies: The student will develop and evaluate various topologies to achieve the desired system accuracy. This involves designing circuits that incorporate pressure sensors, simulating their performance under different conditions, and analyzing the results to ensure the system meets the required accuracy standards. Students will learn to identify potential sources of error and methods to mitigate them.
Optimizing Design Topologies: The student will compare and contrast different design topologies to optimize power consumption, component selection, and manufacturability. This includes evaluating the trade-offs between different design approaches, selecting appropriate components to balance performance and cost, and considering the manufacturability of the design to ensure it can be produced efficiently and reliably.
 
Class Summary:
This course provides an in-depth exploration of precision pressure sensor systems, focusing on the design, modeling, and analysis required to achieve high accuracy and efficiency. Students will learn to understand and utilize key parameters from pressure sensor data sheets to create accurate models in MPLAB Mindi. They will develop and evaluate various circuit topologies to meet desired accuracy standards, simulating sensor performance under different conditions, analyzing results, and identifying potential sources of error and mitigation strategies. Additionally, students will compare and contrast different design approaches to optimize power consumption, component selection, and manufacturability, balancing performance and cost, selecting appropriate components, and ensuring the design can be efficiently and reliably produced. By the end of the course, students will have a comprehensive understanding of how to design and analyze precision pressure sensor systems, equipping them with the skills needed to optimize these systems for various applications.
