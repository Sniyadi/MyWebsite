## Product Purpose

A high-credibility personal portfolio and technical showcase designed to convert recruiter and hiring manager visits into interviews and job opportunities. Success is measured by:
1. Immediate clarity on Sultan's core positioning as an Applications Engineer bridging hardware/silicon expertise with engineering management and AI enablement.
2. Rapid, intuitive access to deep-dive technical project breakdowns (e.g., 4-20mA loop driver, PCB designs, failure analysis) with tangible evidence.
3. Frictionless access to resume/CV download, contact information, and professional profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub).

## Positioning

A distinguished technical portfolio that pairs rigorous hardware and embedded systems expertise (semiconductor customer applications at Microchip Technology, analog/mixed-signal, PCB design, firmware) with strategic engineering management acumen and practical AI-assisted workflow development.
